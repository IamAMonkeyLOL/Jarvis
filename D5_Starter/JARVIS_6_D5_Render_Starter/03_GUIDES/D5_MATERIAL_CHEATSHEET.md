# JARVIS 6 — Creative material recipes (not D5 import presets)

The names below describe *looks to make manually* in D5's materials panel.
They are not machine-readable D5 material files or guaranteed numeric presets.

| Surface | Intended look | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| JARVIS armour | graphite black, metallic satin | soft highlight, controlled reflections |
| Chest reactor & visor | cyan-blue emission | brightest visual points; avoid clipped white |
| Platform rings | blue neon light strips | medium bloom/glow |
| Holographic screens | translucent smoked blue glass | if transparency cannot be mapped, use dark blue glossy |
| Floor | near-black polished concrete | low reflection for mood |
| Walls | steel and dark cobalt | contrast with neon accents |
| Secondary interface lights | violet/indigo | cooler than the reactor |

Suggested palette (hex):
Graphite #0C1927 | Midnight #0D141E | Cyan #1ED6F1 | Violet #7361FC
Ice #C9F4FF | Deep blue #173B75

For hero shot: key ~cyan, rim ~indigo, screens cooler blue. Balance lights by
sight in D5 — power units and controls vary by version.
