# JARVIS 6 — First cinematic D5 render

## 1. Import model
Open D5 Render on Windows > **New** > **Import**. Select
`01_IMPORT_INTO_D5/01_Futuristic_Hologram_Lab.glb`.
If `.glb` is not accepted in your installed D5 release, try the included `.obj`
(with `.mtl` in the same folder), or run the Blender FBX fallback script.
Older D5 articles list fewer accepted direct-import formats than newer docs.

**Check:** full lab floor, elevated central circular platform, JARVIS humanoid,
floating monitors, wall and neon ring visible. Your scene is modeled in metres.

## 2. Make it look like JARVIS (suggested creative settings)
- Environment: choose a dark interior/studio or evening mood; keep sky contribution low.
- Lighting: place a cool cyan/blue key light front-left of JARVIS and a softer
  violet/blue rim light from behind; avoid washing out the face or screens.
- Material: dark graphite floor/armour should be glossy but not a perfect mirror.
  Use moderate roughness, subtle metallic finish on armour and platform trims.
- Glow: identify the cyan/blue imported materials; assign luminous / emissive
  properties in D5's material editor where available. The GLB vertex colours
  may be interpreted differently by different import formats/versions.
- Add fine floor reflections, low-intensity atmospheric fog/haze if available.
- Keep backdrop dark to emphasise reactor/visor and control panels.

## 3. Camera shots (compose manually in D5)
**A — Hero wide (first image):** eye height ~1.7 m, camera on front-right,
looking up slightly toward JARVIS and showing platform + holograms.

**B — JARVIS portrait:** mid-chest to top of head, blue rim lighting, reactor
visible; lower exposure enough to retain visor detail.

**C — Command overview:** import `02_Orbital_Command_Deck.glb` into a SEPARATE
D5 project, and look down slightly on the central consoles/monitors.

**D — Workshop comparison:** import `03_JARVIS_Avatar_Workshop.glb` in a SEPARATE
project, frame the three candidate robot bodies together.

## 4. Export a first image
Try 1920×1080 still render for the hero wide; save to `My_D5_Renders` on your PC.
For testing, reduce preview resolution/settings if your GPU struggles.

## 5. Common issues
- **Everything gray:** reapply materials (the OBJ + MTL materials may need to be
  located / reassigned; vertex colors in GLB are not guaranteed to translate).
- **Black scene:** add lights or raise environment illumination temporarily.
- **Tiny / giant scene:** check import scale/units, start with metre scale 1:1.
- **Too slow / crash:** verify your GPU and VRAM meet D5 requirements; update
  driver and reduce scene/reflections/render settings.
- **No GLB option:** use OBJ+MTL or generate FBX using Blender.
- **OBJ missing colors:** verify the `.mtl` is beside the `.obj` file.

## 6. Save carefully
Save a real D5 project from D5 itself; it cannot be generated by these asset
files alone. Keep the original imported models in place after linking them.
